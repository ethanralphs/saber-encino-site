Questions

Do I need an appointment?+

Never — walk-ins are always welcome at our Encino barber shop. To skip the wait, check in online before you head over and we'll have a chair ready.

Every men's cut includes a precision cut and style finish. Add a shampoo and hot towel finish for $38. Come back within 30 days of your visit and we'll give you a free ear and neck trim to hold you over until your next cut.

Do you cut kids' and seniors' hair?+

Yes. Kids' and senior haircuts are both $30, and we're happy to take our time with first-timers.

What are your hours?+

Monday through Friday 9–7, Saturday 9–6, and Sunday 10–5. Open every day of the week.
