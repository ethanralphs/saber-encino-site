Haircuts for Men in Encino: A Straightforward Guide

Published September 14, 2026 · 3 min read

If you've typed "haircuts for men" into a search bar recently, you've probably already seen the same dozen stock photos and the same vague advice about "finding your face shape." Not super useful. So here's something more practical — what men actually ask for when they come into our shop on Ventura Blvd, what the experience looks like, and how to make sure you walk out with something you actually like.

The cuts we see most often

Fades are still the most popular thing we do — high, mid, low, skin, you name it. A lot of guys pair them with a textured top or a longer, more relaxed style up top so there's some versatility day to day. Classic tapers are a close second, especially for guys who want something clean for work without committing to anything too dramatic on the sides. Longer cuts — think a trim that keeps the shape without taking off much length — come in third, usually from guys who are growing something out and just want it to not look messy in the meantime.

If you're not sure what you want, a photo on your phone goes a long way. Seriously, it's the fastest way to skip the back-and-forth and make sure everyone's on the same page before the clippers come out. Just pull up something close to what you're going for — doesn't have to be perfect.

We also do beard trims, neck trims, and ear trims if you need cleanup around the edges. The MVP Experience adds a hot steamed towel and massaging shampoo to your cut, which a lot of regulars have started tacking on — it's one of those things that sounds optional until you try it once.

What to expect when you come in

Walk-ins are always welcome at 17200 Ventura Blvd — no appointment needed. If you want to cut down on wait time, you can check in online before you leave the house, which lets you see roughly where you are in the queue before you walk through the door. Either way works fine.

The consultation happens at the chair. Tell your barber what you want, show the photo if you've got one, and say if there's anything you definitely don't want. The whole thing — cut, any add-ons — usually runs well under an hour. Pricing depends on the service, and you can find the full list on our pricing page if you want to know what to expect before you come in.

If you've been putting off a haircut because you're not sure what to ask for, that's honestly the most common reason guys wait too long. Just come in, say "I'm not sure, what do you think?" — we've had that conversation plenty of times and it usually leads to a pretty good result.
