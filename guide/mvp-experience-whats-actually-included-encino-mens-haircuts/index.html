The MVP Experience — What You Actually Get

Published October 19, 2026 · 2 min read

If you've sat down in the chair here and been asked "do you want to add the MVP today?" and weren't totally sure what that meant, you're not alone. It comes up a lot. So here's a straightforward breakdown of what's actually included, because it's genuinely worth knowing before you say yes or no.

What's in the MVP Experience

The MVP adds three things on top of your haircut: a massaging shampoo, a hot steamed towel, and a neck and shoulder massage. That's the whole thing — no mystery, no upsell inside the upsell.

The shampoo comes first, before the cut. It's not a quick rinse — it's an actual scalp massage, which honestly feels better than it sounds if you've never had one in a barbershop setting. The hot steamed towel comes at the end and goes around your neck and face, which helps open things up after the cut and just feels good. The massage wraps it up — nothing intense, just a few minutes on the neck and shoulders while you're still in the chair.

Start to finish, the whole MVP service adds maybe 10–15 minutes to your visit. If you're on a lunch break and tight on time, that's worth factoring in. If you've got a little room, most guys find it's the better way to go.

Is it worth adding?

That depends on what you're after. If you just need a quick, clean cut and you're out the door — the regular haircut is exactly that. No pressure either way.

But if you've had a long week, or your neck and shoulders have been carrying some tension (and whose aren't), the MVP is a real difference. A lot of regulars here on Ventura Blvd make it their default just because the shampoo alone makes the cut look cleaner — starting with wet, freshly washed hair gives the stylist a better canvas to work with.

It's also just a nicer 20 minutes than most people give themselves on a random Tuesday or Saturday morning. Worth trying at least once to see if it's your thing.

If you want to try it, just mention it when you check in — walk in anytime or check in online before you come so your wait is shorter. Either way works.
