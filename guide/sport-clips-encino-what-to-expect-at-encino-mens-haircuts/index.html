Sport Clips Encino — Here's What You'll Find at Our Shop

Published August 3, 2026 · 2 min read

If you've been searching for Sport Clips in Encino, you've found us — we're Encino Men's Haircuts, your local Sport Clips franchise right here in the Valley. Whether you're a regular who's been sitting in our chairs for years or you're trying us out for the first time, here's a straightforward rundown of what we're all about and what you can expect when you walk through the door.

The Services We Actually Do Here

We specialize in men's and boys' haircuts, full stop. That means haircuts for adults, seniors, and kids, plus the kind of finishing details that actually make a cut look sharp when you leave: neck trims, ear trims, and beard trims. If you want to add on a massaging shampoo or a hot steamed towel, those are easy upgrades that take maybe ten minutes and feel pretty great — especially after a long week. Our MVP Experience bundles those extras together, and a lot of our regulars have made it their default. You can always just ask whoever's cutting your hair and they'll walk you through it without any pressure.

One thing we do not do: shaving services. We're a licensed barbershop for cuts and trims, not a traditional shave shop. So if you're looking for a straight razor shave, that's not something we offer — but everything else on the haircut side, we've got covered.

How Check-In Works (It's Simple)

No appointment needed. Walk-ins are always welcome, which is honestly how most of our guys show up — they're running an errand in Encino and decide it's time for a cut. That said, if you'd rather skip any wait, you can check in online before you leave the house. It puts you in the queue remotely, so by the time you pull into the parking lot your wait is usually pretty short. Either way, you're not locked into a specific time slot or stylist — just show up, get your hair cut, and get on with your day. If you have questions before you come in, give us a call at (818) 290-3522 and we'll sort it out.

Why Guys from Encino Keep Coming Back

We're not going to oversell it. The reason most of our clients are regulars is pretty simple: consistent cuts, a comfortable shop, and stylists who remember what you asked for last time. The Valley has no shortage of places to get a haircut, but there's something to be said for a shop where you don't have to re-explain your fade or your part every single visit. We're a Sport Clips location, so you get the brand's reliability — but we're locally owned and operated, so it still feels like your neighborhood shop. Come check us out and see for yourself.
